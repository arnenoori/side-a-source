# Side A 0.5.55

- Settings search forgives typos, ranks the closest match first, and works from the keyboard.
- Side A recovers by itself from more failures: a stalled update, an unreadable library file, a pool that didn't start, and a missed notification registration.
- A refused security key offers Try Again, so a mistaken Deny no longer means pairing every iPhone again.
- Remove Side A moved to a right-click on Quit in the menu.

With Side A Remote 1.0.3, the iPhone app says what is wrong instead of showing a blank page, keeps what you type, and no longer gets stuck on "update", "Starting…" or "Sending…".

macOS 14 or later. Apple Silicon and Intel. Free.
