# Side A 0.5.57

- Side A now sends "Please continue" to a session that stopped on something it can get past, once it can: after Autopilot moves its login, after its limit resets, after the pool had every account out and one has room again, and a minute after a response was cut off by sleep or a dropped connection. Only idle sessions are asked, at most three times, and each ask is written to autopilot.log. "Continue sessions once a limit lifts" in Autopilot's settings turns it off.
- New and experimental: Side A can type into a session's own terminal tab when there is no other way to reach it, in Ghostty 1.4 or later and in panes of your own tmux. Continuing a stopped session uses it, and so do replies from the Sessions window. What is typed arrives as your own message. macOS asks once whether Side A may control Ghostty. In Ghostty a draft already in the message field can't be seen, so it would be sent along. Terminal, iTerm, Warp, kitty and WezTerm tabs are not typed into.
- Tokens per day in Usage fits any number of models: it draws the five most used and folds the rest into Other, each with its own color, and the legend wraps instead of running past its card. Its bars now sit on their own day in time zones west of Greenwich.
- Claude Code's newer wording for a spent 5-hour window is recognized, so a session stopped on it marks its account as full at once.

macOS 14 or later. Apple Silicon and Intel. Free.
