# Side A 0.5.39

- Show terminal reaches sessions in your own tmux: Side A brings forward the tab attached to the session, or opens one that attaches.
- Automated runs (`claude -p` from launchd, cron or a script) say what started them instead of offering a terminal they don't have.
- Autopilot can answer questions with their recommended options, and approve plans, after a minute. Permission requests are never answered this way.
- Renaming a session renames the running Claude Code session too.
- A request answered anywhere leaves the island, notifications and the phone at once.
- New Mac logins join your accounts on their own, and Add Account no longer needs a name.

macOS 14 or later. Apple Silicon and Intel. Free.
