# Side A 0.5.48

- Updates install by themselves while you're away from the Mac. Settings has one switch for it.
- Your iPhone buzzes less: nothing while you're at the Mac, a short wait before requests, and one grouped Ready notification.
- Pool mode keeps working when Side A is closed.
- A calmer Mac app: less text, the same names as on iPhone, an island that opens only when you mean it.
- Side A Remote: no banners while the app is open, and replies you already dealt with stay dealt with.
- Fixes for accounts whose names contain words like "idle" or "429".

macOS 14 or later. Apple Silicon and Intel. Free.
