# Side A 0.5.56

- Sign Out waits for Claude or Codex to sign out and says when it didn't happen, instead of marking the account signed out anyway.
- A paired device can attach only the photos it uploaded itself.
- Notification registration retries quickly again after it has once succeeded.
- Messages sent to Side A Remote on an iPad no longer call it an iPhone.

With Side A Remote 1.1, the app runs on iPad, the + button opens a drawer with Camera, Photos, Files and Paste, and New Session takes photos. Scrolling up a long transcript no longer freezes the app, and replies no longer get stuck on "Sending…".

macOS 14 or later. Apple Silicon and Intel. Free.
