# Side A 0.4.1

- Simpler setup: installed tools collapse to one line, sign-in verifies when you return, and the player highlights what to press next.
- Temporary tool failures no longer mark a connected account as signed out. Reconnecting preserves your Auto flip choice.
- Save a small, private diagnostic summary from Settings or Help. Nothing is uploaded.
- Optional usage statistics now count completed setup after your consent choice.

macOS 14 or later. Apple Silicon and Intel. Free public preview.

Claude and Codex sessions must be started from Side A. Codex and Auto flip remain experimental; real multiple-account handoffs are still undergoing acceptance testing.
