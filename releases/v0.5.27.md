# Side A 0.5.27

- Memory per session, counting everything it started, plus the ports it listens on. Stop a server or quit a session from Sessions.
- Quit Idle frees sessions idle for 30+ minutes in one click.
- A warning in the menu, and a notification, when sessions fill your Mac's memory.
- Setup tab: your CLIs, MCP servers, hooks, skills and agents at a glance.

macOS 14 or later. Apple Silicon and Intel. Free.
