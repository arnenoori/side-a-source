# Side A 0.5.62

- The Sessions window shows what the agent did, step by step, the way Side A Remote does: what each step was, whether it worked, how long it took, and what it printed. Edits show the lines taken out and put in, and a finished turn folds behind "Worked for 4m".
- Starting a session is instant: New Session closes when you press Start and the session is in the list while it boots. New Session leads with the prompt, starts in any folder, can cut a worktree from a branch, and can start Claude and Codex together on one prompt.
- Everything in the Sessions window works from the keyboard: ⌘K goes to any session or acts on the open one, ⌘1 to ⌘9 open the top sessions, and the new Session menu lists the rest.
- Changes shows uncommitted work, the whole branch, or each commit. Click a line to comment on it, and Add to Message sends your comments to the agent.
- A hosted session can move to another of your computers and keep running there, with its work and its login. Link computers in Settings > Remote > Computers, then use Move to on the Mac or the iPhone. Standby keeps checkpoints on a linked computer in case this Mac disappears.
- Sessions can be snoozed, archived and quit with Undo, and several rows can be selected at once.
- ⌘S stashes what you have typed in a message field; Edit from Here branches a Claude conversation from any message you sent.
- A session stopped by a usage limit says when it continues, and Codex sessions are now sent on when their account has room again.
- A project can list its commands in `side-a.json`; they appear under Run beside a conversation.
- A reply from your iPhone to a Claude session in your own tmux no longer stops at a draft in its prompt, and a reply left typed but unsent goes in when you retry it.
- On Linux, `sidead` takes sessions moved from a Mac, sends them back, keeps standby checkpoints, and sends Claude Code's permission requests to your iPhone after `sidead hooks on`.

macOS 14 or later. Apple Silicon and Intel. Free.
