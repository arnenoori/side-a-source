# Side A 0.5.54

- A session you start from your iPhone also opens a terminal tab on your Mac, and any session Side A runs has Show on Mac.
- Replies from your iPhone reach idle and ended sessions: an idle one moves into Side A, an ended one resumes.
- Subagents get their own cards in the iPhone transcript, and Side A Remote asks for Face ID once every five minutes.

The iPhone changes need Side A Remote 1.0.3.

macOS 14 or later. Apple Silicon and Intel. Free.
