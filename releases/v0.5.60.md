# Side A 0.5.60

- Messages queued from Side A Remote are no longer stuck. A session that ended its turn while background tasks were still running never released what you queued, and "send now" did nothing. Queued messages now go a few seconds after the turn pauses, or come back to the phone as "Didn't send" with their text.
- Side A Remote uses far less data. A long session opens in tens of kilobytes where it took hundreds, reopening or reconnecting fetches only what is new, and responses are compressed.
- A message you send from the phone shows once in the transcript.
- Scrolling a long session on the phone no longer goes blank for a screen when it refreshes.
- Pool mode uploads far less: the conversation Claude Code resends on every turn is compressed on its way to Anthropic, to about two fifths of its size.
- The phone moves onto a direct connection to the Mac more readily, and reconnects over the relay a second faster when no direct address answers.
- `remote.log` says what happened to each message from the phone and how much data each connection moved.

Side A Remote's next build asks for Face ID when it opens, not after you send, no longer crashes when you swipe Quit, and keeps a session you start as one row in Working.

macOS 14 or later. Apple Silicon and Intel. Free.
