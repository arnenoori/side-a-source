# Side A 0.5.20

- Faster moves at a limit: when a session hits a limit, Side A reads it from the session itself and moves its account right away, even when the usage endpoint is throttled.
- Better targets: Autopilot moves to an account with room on every limit, so it doesn't land on one that is about to run out.
- Early resets: a limited account is checked hourly, so a limit that resets early is used.
- Fixes a crash when Side A posted a notification.
- `source ~/.zshrc && claude` now uses the chosen account.

Thanks to brandedDaniel, whose fork found most of these.

macOS 14 or later. Apple Silicon and Intel. Free.
