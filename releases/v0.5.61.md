# Side A 0.5.61

- The menu no longer says "Update didn't install" under "Updated to" when the Mac is already on the new version.
- Pool mode notices a limit that reset early, such as a weekly reset you redeemed: the account takes requests again as soon as a reading shows the limit emptied, and when every account is resting the pool tries one again every so often.
- An error about an account or pool mode goes away once that account signs in, renews or switches, or pool mode starts. An account you signed in to again no longer keeps its "Sign in again" label beside a working Use button.
- A Claude Code installed with npm (in `~/.npm-global/bin`) is found when you sign in to an account.
- The Sessions window says "Can't Open This Conversation" only for the conversation that failed to load, not for other errors.
- The red error under Remote settings goes away once the thing that failed works again.
- The Mac no longer says "Reconnecting…" about a phone whose connection dot is still green.
- The folder that holds the pool's key is closed to other accounts on the Mac.
- Side A for Linux is out, with Windows through WSL 2 as a preview: `curl -fsSL https://getsidea.com/install.sh | sh`. See getsidea.com/linux and getsidea.com/windows.

With Side A Remote 1.3: the terminal says why it closed instead of "Process Exited", a request you answered no longer flips back to "Needs you", and a session's memory reads the same on the phone as on the Mac.

macOS 14 or later. Apple Silicon and Intel. Free.
