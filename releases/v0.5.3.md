# Side A 0.5.3

- Lives in the menu bar: the active account's usage as a percentage, and every account's 5-hour and weekly limits one click away. Accounts at their weekly limit fold away.
- Autopilot picks the Claude account whose quota expires soonest, weighted by plan size, and warms idle 5-hour windows ahead of your usual hours.
- Read-only by design: Side A never writes or refreshes a login. Turn on Switch in Terminal to point new `claude` commands at the chosen account; an optional hook rechecks the moment a limit hits.
- Tokens by day, project and model, read from local transcripts.
- Codex accounts are tracked and warmed; switch them with `codex login`.
- The menu shows when an update is ready and restarts into it in one click.
- Rename accounts, a single Settings window, and a short guided tour.
- Removed per-project Terminal sessions.

macOS 14 or later. Apple Silicon and Intel. Free public preview.
