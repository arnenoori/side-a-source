# Side A 0.5.33

- A question, plan or permission request you answer in the terminal leaves the island within about a second, instead of staying until the session quits.
- Show terminal folds the request into the notch while you answer there; Answer in terminal brings that terminal forward.
- Finished sessions you quit leave the island's Done list, and "waiting for you" banners and notifications clear once the session moves on.

macOS 14 or later. Apple Silicon and Intel. Free.
