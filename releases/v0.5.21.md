# Side A 0.5.21

- Sessions: a new Settings tab searches every Claude Code and Codex conversation on this Mac, by what you asked, what came back and the commands that ran.
- One list: what needs you first, then your pinned conversations, then each project's, with branches under the conversation they came from. New conversations from the terminal appear within a second.
- Fast: the list is there the moment the tab opens, conversations open in milliseconds, and a search hit opens right at the message.
- Show or resume: a running conversation's button takes you to its terminal tab; others resume with `claude --resume` or `codex resume` in Ghostty, iTerm, Warp or Terminal.
- ⌘F finds within a conversation. Pin, rename or archive conversations without touching Claude's or Codex's files.
- Optional notification when a session needs you, even with Side A closed. Turn it on in Settings > General.
- Soft sound effects when the account changes, a session resumes or one needs you, with a volume setting.

Also in this update, from 0.5.20:

- Faster moves at a limit: when a session hits a limit, Side A reads it from the session itself and moves its account right away, even when the usage endpoint is throttled.
- Better targets: Autopilot moves to an account with room on every limit.
- A limited account is checked hourly, so a limit that resets early is used.
- Fixes a crash when Side A posted a notification, and `source ~/.zshrc && claude` uses the chosen account.

Thanks to brandedDaniel, whose fork found most of the 0.5.20 fixes.

macOS 14 or later. Apple Silicon and Intel. Free.
