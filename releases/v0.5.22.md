# Side A 0.5.22

- One copy at a time: opening Side A while another copy runs hands over to the one you opened, so two copies never trade logins or install updates at once.
- Move to Applications: opened from Downloads or a disk image, Side A offers to move itself where updates can install.
- If an update doesn't install, the menu says so and links the download.
- Settings > General shows the session search index's size with Rebuild, and Remove Side A undoes everything it set up and moves it to the Trash.
- Catching up on your conversation history runs at background priority, so the first index never makes your Mac feel busy.
- In Sessions, a message's time and copy button no longer cover the text around it.

macOS 14 or later. Apple Silicon and Intel. Free.
