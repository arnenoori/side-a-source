# Side A 0.5.59

- "Quiet while I'm watching" in Side A Remote now does what it says: a notification is held only while your phone is in front with that session open, and a request still waiting is sent once you look away.
- A notification that fails to go is tried again, up to three times. Taking a notification away can no longer arrive before the notification itself.
- A request nobody answered no longer turns into "Answered" on your phone. The notification says what happened, such as "Handed back to the terminal".
- The limit notification says the percentage, such as "Work is at 92%".
- A "Didn't send" notice is no longer silent.
- Mac's Screen: a phone that is refused now gets the reason (Screen is off, or Screen Recording isn't allowed) where it used to retry forever.
- `remote.log` says why a notification did or didn't go.

Side A Remote is redesigned in its next build: two tabs and a compose button, approvals on the row and above the message field, and one Face ID while you use it, not one per message.

macOS 14 or later. Apple Silicon and Intel. Free.
