# Side A 0.5.45

- Logins are harder to lose: only Anthropic saying a login is invalid ends one.
- Automatic moves no longer stop over a session that is exiting.
- Keys typed as a phone terminal opens are no longer lost.
- A reply that couldn't be sent comes back to the phone that sent it.
- The Mac tells you when a paired iPhone's notification address changes, with Remove Device on the notification.
- Update Side A Remote to build 13 to use it with this version.
